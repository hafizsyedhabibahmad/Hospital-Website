Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common queries about our services and policies

How do I book an appointment?

You can book appointments through multiple channels: online via our website, mobile app, phone call (+91 22 8765 4321), or by visiting our reception. We recommend booking online for faster confirmation.

What documents do I need for registration?

Please bring your government-issued ID proof, insurance details (if applicable), previous medical records, and referral letter (if referred by another doctor). For first-time registration, you'll need to fill a simple form at the reception.

Do you accept insurance claims?

Yes, we have tie-ups with all major insurance providers and TPA companies. Please bring your insurance card, policy details, and pre-authorization form. Our insurance desk will assist you with all documentation and claims processing.

What are the visiting hours?

General visiting hours are from 10:00 AM to 12:00 PM and 4:00 PM to 7:00 PM. ICU visiting is restricted to immediate family for 15 minutes at specified times. Please check with the reception for specific ward timings.

Do you provide telemedicine services?

Yes, we offer comprehensive telemedicine consultations. You can book video consultations with our specialists through our app or website. Prescriptions and lab test orders are provided digitally. Emergency teleconsultations are available 24/7.

How do I access my medical records online?

All patients get access to our patient portal where you can view medical records, test results, prescriptions, and appointment history. Register with your patient ID and mobile number to access the portal. For assistance, contact our medical records department.
