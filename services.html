Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services

How do I book an appointment?

+

You can book an appointment online through our website, via our mobile app, or by calling our 24/7 helpline. Online booking provides instant confirmation and allows you to choose your preferred doctor and time slot.

What insurance plans do you accept?

+

We accept all major insurance providers including HDFC ERGO, ICICI Lombard, Max Bupa, Star Health, and many more. You can check the complete list on our insurance partners page or contact our billing department for assistance.

Do you provide emergency services?

+

Yes, we have a 24/7 emergency department with dedicated trauma care specialists, advanced life support systems, and immediate diagnostic facilities. Our emergency helpline is always active for immediate assistance.

What are your visiting hours?

+

Regular OPD hours are from 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM, Monday to Saturday. Emergency services are available 24/7. Specialist consultation timings may vary, so we recommend checking individual doctor schedules before visiting.
